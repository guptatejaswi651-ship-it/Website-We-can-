Events

Celebrations, competitions and community on campus

Freshers Dandiya Tech Fest Diwali Sports Startup

Freshers

Welcome Speech: The event typically starts with a welcome speech from the principal or senior faculty members, highlighting the importance of the new academic year.

Orientation Activities: New students participate in various orientation activities to familiarize themselves with the college environment and academic programs.

Introduction and Ramp Walk: New students introduce themselves on stage, often participating in a ramp walk showcasing themed outfits.

Mr. and Ms. Fresher Contest: A multi-round competition featuring talent showcases, fun tasks, and a final question-and-answer round with judges to select the best-performing newcomers.

Dandiya Night

Dance and Music: Participants dance in synchronized circles to the rhythmic beats of Garba and Dandiya folk music, often followed by a high-energy DJ or fusion music session.

Traditional Attire: Students and staff dress up in colorful traditional ethnic wear like kediyus, chaniya cholis, and kurtas.

Stalls and Food: Organizing committees usually set up stalls for refreshments, snacks, and colored wooden sticks (dandiya sticks).

Tech Fest

Duration: Typically spans 1 to 3 days on a college campus.

Competitions: Features coding contests, robotics battles, hackathons, bridge-building challenges, and project expos.

Learning Opportunities: Includes hands-on workshops, expert keynote lectures, and corporate sponsor exhibitions.

Networking: Brings together students, academic faculty, and industry professionals to exchange real-world tech ideas.

Diwali Celebration

Lighting Candles: A vibrant tradition that brings student communities together to celebrate the festival of lights.

Rangoli and Diya Decorating: Students create intricate floor patterns using colored powders, rice, and flower petals at campus entrances, accompanied by the traditional lighting of clay diyas.

Traditional Dress Days: Faculty and students dress in traditional Indian attire like sarees and kurtas to add to the festive atmosphere.

Cultural Performances: Events often feature classical or folk welcome dances, musical performances by student clubs, and theatrical skits depicting the significance of the festival.

Sports Events

Opening Ceremony: The event officially begins with a formal march-past by participating teams or student houses, followed by an oath-taking ceremony to uphold sportsmanship, and the symbolic lighting of a sports torch.

The Competitions: Individual and group speed events like 100m, 200m, and 400m sprints, relay races, long jump, high jump, shot put, and javelin throw. High-energy tournaments featuring popular sports such as cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, and kabaddi.

Closing and Award Ceremony: The event concludes with an official prize distribution where winning athletes and teams are awarded trophies, medals, and certificates. A grand champion trophy is usually given to the overall highest-scoring department or house.

A Startup Meetup

Icebreakers and Ideation: The meetup usually kicks off with casual networking. Students grab a coffee, introduce themselves, and share what they are working on or what skills they bring to the table (e.g., coding, marketing, design).

Elevator Pitches: Founders or students with rough ideas get a few minutes to pitch their concepts to the room. This helps them practice their public speaking and find potential co-founders or team members.

Guest Speaker Talks: Successful local entrepreneurs, angel investors, or alumni are often invited to share their startup journeys, offer practical advice, and answer questions about funding, scaling, or avoiding common pitfalls.

Brainstorming and Feedback Sessions: Attendees break into smaller groups to dissect specific business problems. Peers and mentors provide honest, constructive feedback on product ideas, target markets, and business models.
